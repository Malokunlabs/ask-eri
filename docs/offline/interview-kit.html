ERI · Three-interview kit · Offline use only

Open this file directly on your computer. No site connection, network requests, tracking or automatic storage. Notes disappear when you close or reload unless you export them. Use aliases, avoid sensitive merchant details, and keep exported notes private and outside the repository. Recording requires separate agreement. Do not uncheck consent after note-taking without managing the retained notes according to the participant's wishes.

A suggested 30 minutes

  1. 10 minutes: a recent real decision, before showing the product.
  2. 10 minutes: let them read the market brief without explanation.
  3. 10 minutes: missing evidence, purchase approval and a concrete next step.

Ask what was observed, what remains unknown, and what they would investigate. Three interviews do not prove demand, a forecast or pricing.
